# RCS Graphic Chatbot Knowledge (Starter)

Use this file as baseline context for the website chatbot.

## Services
- Business cards, brochures, flyers, posters, banners, and custom print jobs.
- Bulk and repeat orders are supported.
- Design support is available for selected products.

## Checkout and Payment
- Visitors can place orders as guests during checkout.
- Online payment is available when Razorpay is configured.
- WhatsApp-assisted order placement is available for manual confirmation.

## Shipping
- Shipping charge may be free or paid depending on settings and order total.
- Final shipping charge is shown in cart/checkout totals.

## Support Handoff
- If the answer is not in knowledge, bot should ask visitor to contact support team.
- Bot should not invent policy, delivery time, or pricing values.
